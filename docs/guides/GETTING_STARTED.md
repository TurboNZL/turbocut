# Getting started

TurboCut is a light video editor for shorts (9:16, under a minute) that also copes with 16:9 edits up to about twenty minutes. It runs on Android, Windows, Linux and in a browser, and it makes captions for you.

## Make your first short
1. **Import.** Home → *Import video*, **File → Import media**, or the **Media** tab → import. Imports only fill the **Media** bin (make folders there with the folder button). Drag a clip from the bin onto a track, or use its *Add* button. Each bin item has two small grips: one drags only the picture, the other only the sound.
2. **Cut.** Move the playhead and use **Split** (or **S**). Split cuts only what you have chosen: the selected clip (with its linked sound), the selected caption, or, with nothing selected, the track you last clicked. See [Cutting and selecting](LAYOUTS_AND_KEYS.md). **Trim start** and **Trim end** cut to the playhead. **Delete** removes the selection. Picture and sound are linked, so they cut and move together.
3. **Captions.** Captions tab → *Generate captions*. Pick your style first (see [Caption styles and fonts](STYLES_AND_FONTS.md)). Fix any wrong word in the list under the button.
4. **Save.** **File → Save project** (Ctrl+S) keeps the edit as a `.turbocut` file; see [Saving and opening projects](PROJECTS.md).
5. **Export.** The **Export** button at the top (or **Ctrl+E**). The file is an MP4 where the device can encode H.264, otherwise a WebM (see [Export and codecs](EXPORT.md)).

## On a phone
- The playhead stays in the middle and the timeline scrolls under it. Drag the timeline to scrub.
- Tap a clip to select it. Big handles appear on both ends for trimming; drag the clip itself to move it.
- Pinch the timeline to zoom.
- Tap a tab to open the panel as a sheet; tap the open tab to close it.

## On a computer
- The mouse wheel moves along the timeline. Ctrl or Alt + wheel zooms around the pointer; Shift + wheel scrolls the tracks up and down when there are more than fit.
- The **+** in the corner above the track names adds a video or audio track.
- **Shift**-click selects a range of clips, **Ctrl**-click adds or removes one; drag any selected clip to move them all together.
- **Right-click** a clip, a caption or empty track space for split, duplicate, mute, delete and more.
- **N** turns snapping on or off. Every shortcut can be changed in Settings → Keys.

## Where things are
| You want | Go to |
|---|---|
| Keyframes (zoom, position, opacity, blur, pixelate, volume) | **Clip** tab, Keyframes section; **K** adds keys, **,** and **.** jump between them |
| Save, open or start a project | **File** menu at the top: [Projects](PROJECTS.md) |
| A text layer | the **Text** button in the edit bar |
| Another panel layout | Settings → [Layout](LAYOUTS_AND_KEYS.md) |
| Fonts | Settings → Fonts, or Captions → Fonts… |
| Updates | Settings → [Updates](UPDATES.md) |
