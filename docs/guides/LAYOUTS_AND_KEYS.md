# Layouts, shortcuts and snapping

## Layouts
Settings → Layout. Pick a **preset** and it applies at once. Change anything (drag a panel bar, move or split a panel, turn the dock on) and TurboCut makes a **Custom** copy for you, so the presets stay as they were. Your own layouts can be renamed, deleted, exported and imported as small files.

- Presets: *Automatic* (right panel on a wide screen, sheet on a phone), *Panel on the left*, *Tall timeline*, *Bottom sheet*, *Compact*, *Large controls*, and a *Dock* layout with a floating tab bar at the bottom, where Settings is its own tab.
- **Resizing:** drag the bar between the preview and the side panel, between the preview and the timeline, or between two panels. Double-click a bar to reset it.
- **Splitting panels:** the Layout page shows the editor as a sketch; put Clip, Media, Captions and Project in the same panel or in separate ones, in the order you like.
- A narrow window always gets the bottom sheet, because a side panel would crush the preview. It switches back when the window is wide again.

## Settings
Settings is grouped (Editor, Captions and text, App) and has a **search** box at the top: type a word and only the matching settings show, from every page.

## Shortcuts
Settings → Keys lists every action. Click a shortcut to remove it, **Add** to record another, **Reset** to go back. A conflicting key is reported instead of silently stolen. **?** opens the list.

Useful defaults: **Space** play, **S** split, **[** and **]** trim to the playhead, **K** keyframe, **,** and **.** previous and next keyframe of the setting you last touched (each setting, such as Scale or Opacity, has its own prev/next/key buttons and its own row of diamonds in the Clip tab), **N** snapping, **M** mute.

## Cutting and selecting
- Only one thing is selected at a time: a clip **or** a caption. Click empty space on a track, or press **Esc**, to select nothing.
- **Split** (S) cuts at the playhead: the selected clip and its linked picture or sound; the selected caption (between its words, so both halves read right; a one-word caption cannot be split); or, with nothing selected, the clips on the **active track** (the one you last clicked). Click **CC** to make the caption row the active track. Other tracks are left whole.
- **Delete** removes what is selected, never something else.

## Snapping
Clips and edges jump to the playhead and to each other when dragged close. Turn it off with **N**, the magnet button or Settings → General.

## Zoom
Pinch on a touch screen; Ctrl or Alt + mouse wheel on a computer (zooms around the pointer); the zoom buttons in the edit bar. The plain mouse wheel scrolls the timeline sideways; Shift + wheel scrolls the tracks up and down.
